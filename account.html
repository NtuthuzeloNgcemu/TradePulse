AI reading

When you choose "Read with AI" on a file, TradePulse sends that file to Groq, an AI service in the United States, to find trades and written rules. Nothing is sent unless you click it for that file. Results are saved for you to review, and nothing is added to your journal or playbook until you confirm it. See the Privacy Policy.
