Cookie Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This policy explains what TradePulse stores on your device.

What we store

  • Sign-in session: keeps you logged in. It is stored in your browser's local storage by our authentication provider, Supabase. It is needed for the app to work.
  • Your choices: that you've seen the cookie notice, whether the sidebar is collapsed, and which insights you've hidden. These stay on your device only.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from the jsDelivr content network. Like any website you visit, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser downloads the files.

Managing storage

You can clear this information in your browser settings. If you clear it, you will be logged out.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@tradepulse.app.
