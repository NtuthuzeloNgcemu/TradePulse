Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information TradePulse collects, why, where it is processed, and the choices you have. It applies to the TradePulse website and app.

1. Who we are

TradePulse is a trading journal and analytics service operated from South Africa. Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, can be sent to privacy@tradepulse.app. Our Information Officer under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) can be reached at the same address.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, the market you mainly trade, and your password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Trading information you provide: trades you enter or import, notes, rule scores, emotions, playbook rules, and files you upload such as CSV exports, statements, screenshots and documents.
  • Processing records: the status of each file, and for files you choose to read with AI, what the AI found, which model was used, and when.
  • Technical information: basic logs kept by our hosting provider to run and secure the service. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

We do not currently take payments, so we hold no payment details.

3. How we use it

  • To run your account and show you your own journal, analytics, insights and playbook.
  • To read a file with AI, only when you choose "Read with AI" for that file.
  • To send emails you need, such as account confirmation and password reset.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with the law.

Analytics and insights are calculated from your own trades only. Your information is never used in another person's results, and we do not sell it.

4. AI processing

When you choose "Read with AI" for a file, TradePulse sends that file's contents to Groq, Inc., an AI inference provider in the United States, to identify trades and written trading rules. Nothing is sent to Groq unless you start it for that specific file. According to Groq, it does not keep the content of these requests by default, and we have asked it not to retain them. What the AI finds is saved in your account as a suggestion. Nothing is added to your journal or playbook until you review and confirm it. AI output can be wrong, so always check it against your original file.

5. Where your information is stored

Your account, trades and uploaded files are stored with Supabase, our database and file storage provider, in its European Union (Ireland) region. Files you read with AI are processed by Groq in the United States. These are transfers outside South Africa. We use them because these providers keep your information under security and confidentiality commitments, as POPIA requires.

6. Legal basis

We process your information to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent for optional AI reading, for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, and to comply with the law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete individual trades, files and rules at any time. If you delete your account in Account settings, your account, files, trades, rules and AI results are deleted straight away. Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on its normal schedule.

8. Your rights

You can access and correct your information in the app, download a copy from Account settings, and delete it. You can also object to processing, and complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za). To make any other request, email privacy@tradepulse.app.

9. Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider. Database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own records and files. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

TradePulse is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
