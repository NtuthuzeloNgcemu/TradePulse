A few practical answers.

Can I start without broker sync?

Yes. Automatic broker sync isn't available yet. Import a CSV export from your platform, read a statement or screenshot with AI, or add trades by hand.

Does TradePulse give financial advice?

No. TradePulse is a journaling and analytics product. It helps you review your own trading process.

What happens to my files when I use AI?

Only files you choose to "Read with AI" are sent to Groq, an AI service in the United States. What it finds is shown to you first, and nothing is saved to your journal or playbook until you confirm it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I track emotions and rules?

Yes. Each trade can include an emotion, a rule score like 3/4, a setup tag and notes. Analytics show your rule adherence across trades.
